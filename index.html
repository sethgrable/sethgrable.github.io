I am a theoretical physicist with a PhD from the University of Colorado Boulder who likes scuba diving, live music, working on challenging research problems, and teaching. Past work focused on nonperturbative methods for non-Abelian gauge theories. I am currently researching neural networks, focusing on singular learning theory and the bridge connecting Bayesian inference to partition functions.

My published work includes analytic solutions to strongly interacting quantum field theories in regimes where perturbation theory breaks down. This including work on Yang-Mills, QCD, and large-N scalar and fermionic theories in 3+1d.

What draws me to machine learning is the mathematical relationship between breakdowns in QFTs and breakdowns in learning systems. Nonperturbative field theories diverge at one-loop order due to zero modes at quadratic order which represent flat directions in the action. These zero modes are mathematically equivalent to zero eigenvalues in the Fisher information matrix of neural networks, and singular learning theory is the framework that makes this correspondence precise. In QFTs and learning systems alike, these zero modes are features not bugs, and can lead a robust understanding of the system's dynamics.

Research areas

Singular learning theory

Degenerate saddles, zero modes and phase transitions as the shared language of interacting field theory and neural network training.

The Yang–Mills vacuum

Analytic calculations of the 3+1d Yang-Mills vacuum with special consideration of guage fixing and ghost fields in relationship to classical background gauge configurations. sector.

Many-flavor QCD

Semiclassical, continuum treatment of twelve-flavor chiral QCD and the behavior of the Polyakov loop.

Solvable interacting theories

Marginal theories that that are solvable for all couplings at one-loop order, in arbitary dimensions, with associated thermodynamics and finite temperature effects.N.
